4. Privacy & safety

We want everyone to create in a respectful, safer environment.

Community commitments

  • Be respectful. No bullying, threats, hate, or targeted harassment.
  • Do not post another person's address, private messages, or personal details without permission.
  • No dangerous challenges, illegal activity promotion, or age-inappropriate content.
  • Use reporting tools if content or behavior makes you feel unsafe.
  • Protect your login details. CreatorHub staff will never need your password.

Privacy basics

  • Your date of birth and email should never appear on a public profile.
  • Share only information needed for the application. Do not upload IDs or sensitive documents to this demo.
  • Applications should be accessible only to authorized reviewers in a real deployment.
  • Real services need a published privacy notice, secure storage, deletion process, and appropriate protections for young users.
